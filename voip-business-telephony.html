Can employees use their business extension while working remotely?

Yes. DSI's 3CX page highlights mobility features that let employees answer office calls and transfer them while away from their desks using supported apps and devices.

Can a business keep an existing SIP trunk?

The DSI edition summary lists “bring your own SIP trunk” as a feature of 3CX FREE. Confirm trunk compatibility and the available configuration choices with DSI.

Does 3CX include video calls and messaging?

DSI lists video conferencing, live chat and team messaging among 3CX capabilities. Specific channels and administrative features may depend on the edition.

Which 3CX edition should we select?

Start with your user count, number of branches, need for deskphones, routing and queues, integrations and deployment preferences. DSI can help confirm which currently available edition fits.
